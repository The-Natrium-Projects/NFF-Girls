The Swamper is an octopus-like monster found in the Swamp and Mangrove Swamp biomes above Y=64.
<NEWLINE>
It can perform both melee and distant attack. The melee attack can give Poison I and Slowness I. The distant attack
(ink spraying) causes Blindness effect.
<NEWLINE>
The Swamper can breathe underwater, and will suffocate in dry biomes outside water. It's weak to fire damage and
resistant to falling.
<NEWLINE>
When a Swamper is slain, it can drop some Swamper Tentacles. This is a kind of food which can be cooked and made into
a stew. It can also drop some Experience Berries that can give you some XP when eaten.
