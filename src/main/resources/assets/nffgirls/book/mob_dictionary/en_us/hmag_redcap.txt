To get friendly with a Redcap, you must drop the item on the ground. It will pick the item up, watch it for a few seconds
like the Piglin, and consume the item.
<NEWLINE>
It loves axes, the Golden Apple, Golden Tropical Fish and HMaG berries. It also receives the Bread and Wheat.
<NEWLINE>
After getting friendly, you can equip them with armors (except the helmet) and hand items. It has 1 Bauble slot.
<NEWLINE>
Its main hand slot only receives axes. If you're also holding an axe on the main hand, it will provide Haste effect of
level depending on its holding axe: Below Diamond - Haste I; Diamond or equal: Haste II; Above Diamond (including Netherite) - Haste III.
<NEWLINE>
It can be healed with the Apple, Cookie, Pumpkin Pie, Lemon and Lemon Pie. A Golden Apple can fully recover its health.
<NEWLINE>
<NEWLINE>
Mod compat: it receives Maze Map Focus (The Twilight Forest).
