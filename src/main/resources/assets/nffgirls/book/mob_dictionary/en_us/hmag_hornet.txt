To get friendly with a Hornet, you must set up a field of honey before giving items. There must be 8 honey blocks around,
otherwise the friendly progress will drop quickly. If you're attacked by the mob, you'll enter a cool time of 60 seconds
during which you cannot give items.
<NEWLINE>
The Hornet loves the Mysterious Petal and Honey Block. It also receives Honey Bottles and flowers.
<NEWLINE>
After getting friendly, you can equip it with hand items, and it has 2 Bauble slots.
<NEWLINE>
It can be healed with the Honey Bottle, Honeycomb and Honey Block. A Mysterious Petal can fully recover its health.
