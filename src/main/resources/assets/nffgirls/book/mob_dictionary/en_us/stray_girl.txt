The Stray Girl is a common mob which spawns occasionally in place of Strays in the Overworld.
Its behaviors are similar to the vanilla Stray while the attributes are a bit higher.
<NEWLINE>
When a Stray Girl is slain, it has a chance to drop some Evil Crystal Fragments, a basic ingredient for most items in this mod.
