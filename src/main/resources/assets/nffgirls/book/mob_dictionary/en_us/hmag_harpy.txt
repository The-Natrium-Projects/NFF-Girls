To get friendly with a Harpy, you must drop the item on the ground. It will pick the item up, watch it for a few seconds
like the Piglin, and consume the item. As the progress increases, it will get Strength effect. Each 20% progress will
provide 1 level of the effect.
<NEWLINE>
The Harpy's favorite item is the Golden Apple, and also receives the Golden Tropical Fish and cooked meat.
<NEWLINE>
After getting friendly, the Harpy has 4 Bauble slots. It can be healed with cooked meat. A Golden Apple can fully
recover its health.
