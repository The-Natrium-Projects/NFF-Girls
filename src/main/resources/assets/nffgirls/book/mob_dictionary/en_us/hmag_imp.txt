To get friendly with an Imp, you must drop the item on the ground. It will pick the item up, watch it for a few seconds
like the Piglin, and consume the item.
<NEWLINE>
The Imp's favorite items are the Nether Star and Golden Apple, and also receives the Golden Tropical Fish, Gilded Blackstone,
Lemon, Crimson Fungus, Warped Fungus and Apple. When the progress reaches 70%, it will become neutral.
<NEWLINE>
After being friendly, you can put item on its main hand, and it has 2 Bauble slots.
<NEWLINE>
If it has a Netherite Fork on the main hand and a Tiny Netherite Scrap on the off-hand (metal nugget icon), it can locate
the Ancient Debris nearby. If it locates an Ancient Debris, it will consume a Netherite Scrap, try to walk toward the block
and generate a lot of green star particles. It locates the Debris only in the Follow mode.
<NEWLINE>
The Netherite Fork can be obtained by upgrading the Golden Fork with a Netherite Ingot. If you have problems getting a
Golden Fork, you can also make it on the Crafting Table, but it's really expensive.
<NEWLINE>
The Imp can be healed with the Apple, Cookie, Pumpkin Pie, Lemon and Lemon Pie. A Golden Apple can fully recover its health.
<NEWLINE>
<NEWLINE>
(*Note: take care using Ancient Debris locating feature. Now block locating has issues which can cause a waste of metal nuggets.)
