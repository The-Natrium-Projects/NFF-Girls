The Imp is a resident of the Nether, found in the Nether Wastes, the Crimson Forest and the Basalt Delta biomes.
<NEWLINE>
It spawns with a Golden Fork. Its melee attack gives Weakness I and Mining Fatigue I when the difficulty is normal
or above.
<NEWLINE>
As a Nether mob, the Imp is immune to fire damage, and is resistant to falling and magical damages.
<NEWLINE>
When an Imp is slain, it has a chance to drop the Golden Fork on its hand. This is a weapon slightly weaker than the
Golden Sword. It can also drop some rare items like Gilded Blackstone and Bottle o' Enchanting.
