The Ghastly Seeker is a rare mob which only spawns in place of the Ghast in a small chance.
<NEWLINE>
The features of the Ghastly Seeker are similar to vanilla Ghasts, but it has much higher attributes. Besides,
the damage from its own fireball cannot immediately kill it but only deals twice damage.
<NEWLINE>
As a Nether mob, it's immune to fire damage.
