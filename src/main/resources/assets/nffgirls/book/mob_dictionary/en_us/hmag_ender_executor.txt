To get friendly with an Ender Executor, you must first provoke it by looking at its eye. Then you have to give items
while resisting its attack. Note that hiding yourself behind blocks is not an option - the friending process will drop
if it cannot reach you for over 10 seconds. Shield-block the attack instead. Also, getting over 8 blocks away from it will
interrupt the process, and you have to restart from the beginning. This process is incredibly dangerous, and be sure to
prepare well before doing this.
<NEWLINE>
The Ender Executor's favorite item is the Ender Pie. It also accepts the Ender Plasm and Ender Eye.
<NEWLINE>
After being friendly, you can equip items on its hands, and it has 2 Bauble slots. It also has a block slot, and the block
in the slot will be held on its hand, but not giving any bonus in attributes now. Note that the block and off-hand slots
are not compatible.
<NEWLINE>
The Ender Executor can be healed with the Ender Eye and Ancient Stone.
