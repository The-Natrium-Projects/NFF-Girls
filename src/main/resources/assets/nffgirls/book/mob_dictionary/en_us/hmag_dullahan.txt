To get friendly with a Dullahan, you need to give it items by right-clicking. Before giving, you must have an
Undead Affinity effect which can make it neutral. You can get this effect by eating Soul Cake or Soul Cake Slice,
stepping on the Soul Carpet or wearing a Necromancer's Hat.
<NEWLINE>
The Dullahan's favorite item is the Soul Cake Slice. It also receives the Soul Apple and Soul Powder.
<NEWLINE>
Note that once you provoke it during the friending process, no matter how, the process will be interrupted, and you
have to restart from the beginning.
<NEWLINE>
After getting friendly, you can modify its items on hands. It has 2 Bauble slots. Although not undead, it can equip
the Soul Amulet.
<NEWLINE>
The Dullahan can be healed with Rotten Flesh, Bone and Soul Powder. A Soul Apple can fully recover the health.
