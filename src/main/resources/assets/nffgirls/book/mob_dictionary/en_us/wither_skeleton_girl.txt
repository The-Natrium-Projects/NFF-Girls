The Wither Skeleton Girl spawns occasionally in place of the Wither Skeleton in the Nether. Its behaviors are similar to
the vanilla Wither Skeleton while the attributes are higher. Rarely it can spawn with a Bow instead of a Stone Sword,
and the arrows can ignite the target on hit.
<NEWLINE>
When a Wither Skeleton Girl is slain, it has a chance to drop some Evil Crystal Fragments, a basic ingredient for most items in this mod.
