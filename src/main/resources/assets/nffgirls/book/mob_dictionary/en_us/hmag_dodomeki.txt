To get friendly with a Dodomeki, you need to give it items by right-clicking. Before giving, you must have an
Undead Affinity effect which can make it neutral. You can get this effect by eating Soul Cake or Soul Cake Slice,
stepping on the Soul Carpet or wearing a Necromancer's Hat.
<NEWLINE>
The Dodomeki's favorite item is the Soul Cake Slice. It also receives the Soul Apple and Soul Powder.
<NEWLINE>
Note that once you provoke it during the friending process, no matter how, the process will be interrupted, and you
have to restart from the beginning.
<NEWLINE>
After getting friendly, it has 4 Bauble slots. Although not undead, it can equip the Soul Amulet.
<NEWLINE>
It can be healed with the Soul Powder and Soul Apple.
