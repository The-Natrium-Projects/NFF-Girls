To get friendly with a Zombie Girl, you need to give it items by right-clicking. Before giving, you must have an
Undead Affinity effect which makes undead mobs neutral. You can get this effect by eating Soul Cake or Soul Cake Slice,
stepping on the Soul Carpet or wearing a Necromancer's Hat.
<NEWLINE>
The Zombie Girl's favorite item is the Soul Cake Slice. It also receives the Soul Apple and Soul Powder.
<NEWLINE>
Note that once you provoke it during the friending process, no matter how, the process will be interrupted, and you
have to restart from the beginning.
<NEWLINE>
After getting friendly, you can equip it with armors and hand items. It has 2 Bauble slots.
<NEWLINE>
The Zombie Girl can be healed with the Soul Powder and Soul Apple. Like the vanilla Zombie, it will convert to the
Drowned Girl after sinking into water for a few seconds. This conversion can be reverted with a Sponge.
