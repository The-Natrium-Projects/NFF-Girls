The Slime Girl is a creature living in the wet places. They can be found in the Swamp and Mangrove Swamp biomes above
Y=64. Its melee attack gives Slowness II effect.
<NEWLINE>
It gets Regeneration III in water or rain, and is resistant to falling and magic damages.
<NEWLINE>
The Slime Girl has 32 different colors.
<NEWLINE>
When a Slime Girl is defeated, it has a chance to drop the Cubic Nucleus. This is a food which can give Regeneration I
effect. However, it's more useful as an ingredient for making advanced items and the Potion of Regeneration III.
