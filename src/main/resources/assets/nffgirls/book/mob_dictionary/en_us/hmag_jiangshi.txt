To get friendly with a Jiangshi, you must "tame" it in a style of the Taoist.
<NEWLINE>
First you need to prepare some Taoist Talismans. Use a talisman on a Jiangshi will freeze its actions for 10s. When frozen,
it will obtain 20 extra armor value. Then you need to attack it with a Peach-Wood Sword when it's frozen. After attacking,
it will be immediately unfrozen and the talisman has a chance to be recovered. You need overall 6 hits to finish the process.
<NEWLINE>
Each time you hit the Jiangshi, it will become stronger and release more smoke particles. After 3 hits, it will be hit by
a thunderbolt, and it's knock-back will become really strong. After 6 hits, another thunderbolt will strike, and it's finally
tamed.
<NEWLINE>
Besides taming the Jiangshi, the Peach-Wood Sword is also a powerful weapon against the undead mobs. When hit an undead mob,
it will deal damage with an amount 50% of the mob's maximum health, bypassing vanilla damage reducing mechanics. The extra
damage will consume extra durability, and note that its durability is only equal to the Wooden Sword.
<NEWLINE>
Specially, the hostile Jiangshi will not take the extra damage but get Weakness II effect. The friendly Jiangshi will
take this damage.
<NEWLINE>
After getting friendly, the Jiangshi has 4 Bauble slots. When it's thunder-struck, it will not take damage but get powerful
effects, including Strength III, Damage Absorption III and Fire Resistance for 1 minute, and get 20 experience.
<NEWLINE>
Like most undead mobs, it can be healed with the Soul Powder and Soul Apple.
