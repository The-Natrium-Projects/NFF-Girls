The Husk Girl is a common mob which spawns occasionally in place of Zombies in the Desert biomes.
Its behaviors are similar to Vanilla Husks while the attributes are a bit higher.
<NEWLINE>
When a Husk Girl is slain, it has a chance to drop some Evil Crystal Fragments, a basic ingredient for most items in this mod.
