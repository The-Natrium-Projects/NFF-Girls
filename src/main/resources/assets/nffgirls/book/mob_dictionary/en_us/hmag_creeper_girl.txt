To get friendly with a Creeper Girl, first you must right-click to give it items. It loves the Particle of Lightning
best, and also receives the TNT and Gunpowder.
<NEWLINE>
When the progress is full, it will stop moving, swell for 4 second with massive green star and smoke particles.
After the 4 seconds, it will make a HUGE EXPLOSION (power 12). During this process, you must not get over 8 blocks away
from the Creeper Girl, or the process will be interrupted, and you have to restart from giving items. You must survive
from the explosion no matter how, and it will finally get friendly with you.
<NEWLINE>
After getting friendly, you can equip armor and hand items to it. It also has an ammo slot which accepts Gunpowder or TNT.
If it has ammo, it can approach the enemy and perform explosions. Gunpowder cannot break blocks but TNT can. Also,
you can use a Particle of Lightning by Shift + Right-click to charge it, and its next explosion will have double power.
Shift + Right-click your charged Creeper Girl to uncharge it and recover a Particle of Lightning.
<NEWLINE>
Its explosions will not hurt itself, but can hurt any players and mobs. The explosions will not kill the owner's other
monster girls or tamed animals, but can kill its owner.
<NEWLINE>
The Creeper Girl can be healed with the Redstone, Gunpowder and Redstone Block.
