The Hornet is a flying insect creature found in the Forest biomes above Y=64. It spawns with an Iron Spear and rushes
to its prey to perform melee attack. Its attack gives Poison II when the difficulty is normal or above.
<NEWLINE>
The Hornet is arthropod and is weak to Bane of Arthropod enchantment. It's also weak to fire.
<NEWLINE>
When a Hornet is slain, it drops some Honeycombs and Honey Bottles, and also has a chance to drop some Mysterious
Petals. This is a unique ingredient useful to make advanced items like Enchanted Golden Apples. Its Iron Spear also
has a chance to be dropped, a weapon slightly weaker than the Iron Sword.
