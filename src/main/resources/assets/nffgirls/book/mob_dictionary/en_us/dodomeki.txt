The Dodomeki is a monster from the legends of the oriental islands, found in the Taiga biomes above Y=64.
Its melee attack gives Slowness I. It burns under the sun, although it's not undead.
<NEWLINE>
When it's slain, it can drop some Randomberry. This food can give you a random effect for a short time, either positive or negative.
