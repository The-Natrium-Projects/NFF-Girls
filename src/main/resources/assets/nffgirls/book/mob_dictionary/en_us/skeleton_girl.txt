The Skeleton Girl is a common mob which spawns occasionally in place of Skeletons in the Overworld.
Its behaviors are similar to Vanilla Skeletons while the attributes are a bit higher.
<NEWLINE>
When a Skeleton Girl is slain, it has a chance to drop some Evil Crystal Fragments, a basic ingredient for most items in this mod.
