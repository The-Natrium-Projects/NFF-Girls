The Jack o' Frost is a spirit of the ice element, found in snowy biomes above Y=64. It attacks by shooting snow balls
with damage. When it steps onto the water, it can freeze the water like the Frost Walker enchantment.
<NEWLINE>
The Jack o' Frost is weak to the heat and fire damage. It will take damage in hot biomes like the Desert. It can get
Regeneration I when in Powder Snow.
<NEWLINE>
If a Jack o' Frost is slain, it can drop some Experience Berries. This food can give you some XP when eaten.
