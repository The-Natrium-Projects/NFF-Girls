To get friendly with a Wither Skeleton Girl, you need to give it items by right-clicking. Before giving, you must have an
Undead Affinity effect which makes undead mobs neutral. You can get this effect by eating Soul Cake or Soul Cake Slice,
stepping on the Soul Carpet or wearing a Necromancer's Hat.
<NEWLINE>
The Wither Skeleton Girl's favorite item is the Nether Star and Soul Cake Slice. It also receives the Soul Apple and Soul Powder.
<NEWLINE>
Note that once you provoke it during the friending process, no matter how, the process will be interrupted, and you
have to restart from the beginning.
<NEWLINE>
After getting friendly, you can equip it with armors and hand items. It has 2 Bauble slots.
<NEWLINE>
It requires arrows to shoot, and can auto switch between the main and secondary weapons, either bows or melee weapons,
depending on the distance to its enemy.
<NEWLINE>
The Wither Skeleton Girl can be healed with Rotten Flesh, Bone and Soul Powder. A Soul Apple can fully recover the health.
