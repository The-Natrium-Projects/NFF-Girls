The Lich is a powerful undead wizard living deep underground, found in the dark areas under Y=0.
<NEWLINE>
It attacks by shooting magical bullets and can give Slowness I. When the enemy is close nearby, it also summons Vexes
for help. Therefore, it's unadvisable to rashly approach the Lich if your equipment isn't strong enough.
<NEWLINE>
The Lich is resistant to fire, magic and Powder Snow damage. It's undead, but can swim in water.
<NEWLINE>
When a Lich is slain, it drops some Lich Cloth. This is an important ingredient for making many advanced items like the
Cloth of Purification, and Potions of Healing and Harming III.
<NEWLINE>
The Cloth of Purification is a magical item which can remove the curse enchantments on the Smithing Table.
