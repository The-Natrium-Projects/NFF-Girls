The Wither Ghost is a more dangerous variant of the Ghost found in the Nether. Its behaviors are the same as the Ghost
in the Overworld, but the attack can give Wither I effect. Also, it's immune to Wither and fire damage.
<NEWLINE>
The Wither Ghost has 5 different textures.
<NEWLINE>
When a Wither Ghost is slain, it can drop some Soul Powder, a useful ingredient for many items in this mod. It can also drop
Wither Rose occasionally.
