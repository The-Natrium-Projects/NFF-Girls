To get friendly with a Melty Monster, you need to be "Into Fire" before giving items. You must be either in the Lava or
on fire, and NOT having a Fire Resistance effect. Each item given, you'll get Combustion effect for 30s.
<NEWLINE>
The Melty Monster's favorite items are the Nether Star and Burning Core Block (1.19.2+). It also receives the Burning Core,
Blaze Rod and Blaze Powder.
<NEWLINE>
After getting friendly, it has 4 Bauble slots and a Lava Bucket slot. The Lava Bucket slot only receives Lava Bucket,
and changes its behavior. If the Lava Bucket is present, its movement speed will increase on the ground, and will not
try to return to the Lava.
<NEWLINE>
The Melty Monster has a "Heat" property. It will consume "Heat" outside the Lava, and equipping a Lava Bucket can slow the
consuming. The Heat will be restored when it's in Lava. If the Heat runs out, it will be unable to fire, its movement will
slow down, and will try to return to Lava no matter if it's equipping a Lava Bucket. Its maximum Heat will increase while
the XP level boosts.
<NEWLINE>
It attacks by shooting fireballs like the Blaze. Each fireball consumes 5 Heat. When it reaches some specific XP levels,
it will be able to shoot more fireballs in a single attack, and also consume more Heat.
<NEWLINE>
Right click with a Water Bucket to prevent it from igniting its standing block. Use a Flint and Steel to enable igniting.
<NEWLINE>
The Melty Monster can be healed with the Coal, Fire Charge and Blaze Powder. A Burning Core can fully recover its health.
