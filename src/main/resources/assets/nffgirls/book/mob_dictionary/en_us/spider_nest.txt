The Spider Nest is a mob as a nest of Cave Spiders, found in the dark area below Y=0 in the Overworld.
<NEWLINE>
It can summon Cave Spiders besides melee attack, and also spawn Cave Spiders after death. Its melee attack gives
Poison I and Slowness I.
<NEWLINE>
The Spider Nest is immune to Poison, and resistant to projectiles, Cactus and explosions. As an arthropod, it's weak to
fire and Bane of Arthropod enchantment. Its movement is not affected by the Cobweb.
<NEWLINE>
When a Spider Nest is slain, it can drop some Experience Berries. This food can give you some food. It may also drop
some rare items like the Bottle o' Enchanting.
