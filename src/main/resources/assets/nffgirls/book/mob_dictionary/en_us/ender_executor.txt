The Ender Executor spawns occasionally in place of the Ender Man in the. They are rare in the Overworld and the
Nether, but common in the End.
<NEWLINE>
It's health and attack attributes are much higher than the Ender Man. It spawns with a Stone Sword, and other
features are similar to the Ender Man.
<NEWLINE>
Besides melee attack, the Ender Executor can also perform beam attack like the Guardian. The beam has very long tracking
distance, but can be stopped by solid blocks.
<NEWLINE>
Therefore, it's unadvisable to rashly provoke an Ender Executor.
<NEWLINE>
When it's slain, it has a chance to drop some Evil Crystal Fragments, a basic ingredient for most items in this mod.
<NEWLINE>
It can also drop some Ender Plasm. This unique ingredient can be used to make advanced items like Endless Pearl
and the Potion of Ender Rage.
<NEWLINE>
The Endless Pearl allows you to infinitely throw Ender Pearls. Besides saving the inventory space, you can throw the
pearls very far away when holding Shift button, which is very useful for jumping between the outer islands in the End.
<NEWLINE>
The Ender Rage effect makes a player or mob proactively attacked by Ender Men and Ender Executors. Higher level can
increase the radius of provoking. Ender Men and Ender Executors are immune to this effect.
