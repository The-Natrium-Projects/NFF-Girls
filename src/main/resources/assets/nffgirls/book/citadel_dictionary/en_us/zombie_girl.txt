The Zombie Girl is a common mob which spawns occasionally in place of Zombies in the Overworld.
Its behaviors are similar to Vanilla Zombies while the attributes are a bit higher.
<NEWLINE>
When a Zombie Girl is slain, it has a chance to dropsome Evil Crystal Fragments, a basic ingredient for most items in this mod.
