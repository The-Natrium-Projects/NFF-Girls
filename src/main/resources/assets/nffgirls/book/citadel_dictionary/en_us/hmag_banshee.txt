To get friendly with a Banshee, first you need to arrange a field of wither. You're required to be in the Wither effect,
and there must be over 8 Wither Roses around you on the ground. Then you can right click to give it items.
<NEWLINE>
Note that the friending progress will drop quickly when there aren't enough Wither Roses. If you're hit by the mob,
you'll enter a cooling time of 60 seconds during which you cannot give any items to it.
<NEWLINE>
Like most undead mobs, the Banshee loves Soul Cake Slices best. It also receives Soul Apples and Soul Powders.
<NEWLINE>
After getting friendly, you can put items on its hands, and it has 3 Bauble slots. Note that the item on its off-hand
cannot be stacked no matter what it is.
<NEWLINE>
If it's holding a flower on the off-hand, it will provide effects depending on the flower type, same as the corresponding
Suspicious Stew's effect. If the effect is positive, it will apply to the owner and allies within 8 blocks around;
the negative effects will be applied on the enemy when hit.
<NEWLINE>
Like most undead mobs, it can be healed with the Soul Powder and Soul Apple.
