The Drowned Girl is a common mob which spawns occasionally in place of the Drowned in the Overworld.
Its behaviors are similar to the Vanilla Drowned while the attributes are a bit higher.
<NEWLINE>
When a Drowned Girl is slain, it has a chance to dropsome Evil Crystal Fragments, a basic ingredient for most items in this mod.
