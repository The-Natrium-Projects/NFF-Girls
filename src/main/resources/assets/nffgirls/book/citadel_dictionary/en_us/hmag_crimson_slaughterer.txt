To get friendly with a Crimson Slaughterer, first you must suppress its hostility with Warped blocks before giving items.
It must be standing on a Warped block. If so, it will get Slowness I effect, but the attack knockback will become very
strong. Also, there must be >16 Shroomlight blocks around. If there aren't enough, the friending progress will drop
quickly.
<NEWLINE>
The Crimson Slaughterer loves the Nether Star, Netherite Ingot and Golden Apple best. It also receives the Shroomlight,
Nether Wart, Crimson Fungus, Crimson Roots and Weeping Vines.
<NEWLINE>
After being friendly, it has 4 Bauble slots.
<NEWLINE>
If you're close to your Crimson Slaughterer, it will give you 1 hunger value each 10 seconds. Specially, if you're
holding a Crimson Bow, it doesn't give but removes 1 hunger value until the hunger reaches 7, allowing you to make
more damage with the bow.
<NEWLINE>
The Crimson Slaughterer can be healed with the Crimson Fungus, Nether Wart and Shroomlight. A Golden Apple can fully
recover its health.
