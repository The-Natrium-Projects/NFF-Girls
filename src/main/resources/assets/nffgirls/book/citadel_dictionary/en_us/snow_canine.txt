The Snow Canine is an animal living in the cold places. It's found in the Snowy Plains biomes above Y=64.
<NEWLINE>
It attacks with a strong knockback, and is resistant to falling damage. It actively hunts Sheeps and Rabbits.
<NEWLINE>
If a Snow Canine is slain, it can drop some Sharp Fangs. This is a unique ingredient for making some items like Reinforcing
Chain, and also as the arrow head.
