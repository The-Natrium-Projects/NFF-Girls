To get friendly with a Cursed Doll, you must fix its hurt with Wools and Strings. Give a Wool followed by a String
to finish a "sewing" action. You need 8 such actions in total with a Wool of different color every time.
<NEWLINE>
During this process, the Cursed Doll will keep hostile to you. If you're attacked, it will randomly drop a Wool you've
given, and the corresponding "sewing" action will be cancelled.
<NEWLINE>
After getting friendly, you can put items on its hands, and it has 6 Bauble slots. Although not undead, it can equip the
Soul Amulet.
<NEWLINE>
The Cursed Doll can be healed with the String, Lapis Lazuli, Emerald and White/Black/Gray/Light-Gray/Brown Wools. A
Cubic Nucleus can fully recover its health. Wools of other colors cannot heal the mob, but give different effects with a
shared cool time of 15s. Notably, the Pink Wool can remove all its negative effects.
