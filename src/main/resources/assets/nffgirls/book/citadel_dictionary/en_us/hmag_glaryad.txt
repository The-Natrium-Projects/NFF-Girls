To get friendly with a Glaryad, you need to right-click to give it items. To give, the mob must have Regeneration III
effect for over 10 seconds, meaning you need Splash Potion of Regeneration III to satisfy the condition. Also, you must
hold a Mysterious Petal on the off-hand. Note that you must not have provoked the mob before.
<NEWLINE>
The Glaryad's favorite items are Cureberries, Randomberries, XP Berries and Mysterious Petals, and also receives
Spore Blossoms and other flowers.
<NEWLINE>
After being friendly, the Glaryad has 3 Bauble slots. It can be healed with the Wheat Seed, Bone Meal and Spore Blossom.
A Mysterious Petal can fully recover its health.
