To get friendly with an Alraune, you need to right-click to give it items. To give, the mob must have Regeneration III
effect for over 10 seconds, meaning you need Splash Potion of Regeneration III to satisfy the condition.
<NEWLINE>
The Alraune's favorite items are Cureberries, Randomberries, XP Berries and Mysterious Petals, and also receives
Spore Blossoms and other flowers.
<NEWLINE>
After being friendly, it has 3 Bauble slots. It can shoot Poisonous seeds to the enemy, cause Poison I, Weakness I
and Slowness I effects. The seed doesn't affect the owner, the owner's other monster girls and tamed animals.
<NEWLINE>
When it sees an ally with health <50%, it can shoot a healing seed, providing healing by 1 and Regeneration I effect.
<NEWLINE>
The Alraune can be healed with the Wheat Seed, Bone Meal and Spore Blossom. A Mysterious Petal can fully recover its health.
