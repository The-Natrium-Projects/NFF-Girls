The Necrostalker is an undead creature living in the darkness, found below Y=0 in the overworld. It attacks with its
scythe-shaped arms and give Slowness I.
<NEWLINE>
As an undead mob, it burns under the sun.
<NEWLINE>
When a Necrostalker is slain, it has a chance to drop some Necrofibre. This unique ingredient can be used to make the
Necrotic Chainmail suit.
<NEWLINE>
The Necrotic Chainmail suit is an upgrade of the Chainmail suit, providing extra attacking speed.
