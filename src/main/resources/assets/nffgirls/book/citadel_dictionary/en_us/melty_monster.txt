The Melty Monster is a spirit of the fire element, found in the lava in the Overworld and Nether.
<NEWLINE>
It attacks by shooting Fire Charges like the Blaze. When a Melty Monster steps onto a solid block, it will ignite the block.    岩浆娘攻击方式类似烈焰人，会连续
<NEWLINE>
The Melty Monster is immune to fire, and takes damage in water. It escapes from the Strider and is neutral to players
riding a Strider.
<NEWLINE>
When a Melty Monster is slain, it drops some Magma Cream, and also possibly Evil Crystal Fragments. This is a basic
ingredient for most items in this mod.
