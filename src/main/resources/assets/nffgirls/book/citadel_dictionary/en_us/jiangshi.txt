The Jiangshi is an undead creature from the eastern continent, found in the Bamboo Jungle biomes above Y=64. It hops
toward the enemies and performs melee attack. Its attack has a strong knockback and, when the difficulty is normal or
above, gives Poison I.
<NEWLINE>
When a Jiangshi is damaged, its movement speed will be boosted, and return to normal gradually. If on fire or hit by
the thunder, the speed will immediately return to normal.
<NEWLINE>
It's resistant to projectiles. As an undead mob, it burns under the sun.
<NEWLINE>
If a Jiangshi is slain, it can drop some Sharp Fangs. This is a unique ingredient for making some items like Reinforcing
Chain, and also as the arrow head. It can also drop some Soul Powder, a useful ingredient for many items in this mod.
<NEWLINE>
It has 2 different textures.
