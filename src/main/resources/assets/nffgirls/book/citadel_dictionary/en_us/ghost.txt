The Ghost is a hostile spirit in the darkness, and is common in the Overworld. It floats in the air, approachs the player
slowly, and rushes to the player to perform melee attack.
<NEWLINE>
Sometimes, the Ghost can spawn with a golden tool or weapon. It's undead and burns under the sun.
<NEWLINE>
It has 5 different textures.
<NEWLINE>
When a Ghost is slain, it can drop some Soul Powder, a useful ingredient for many items in this mod. It can also drop
Glowstone Powder occasionally.
