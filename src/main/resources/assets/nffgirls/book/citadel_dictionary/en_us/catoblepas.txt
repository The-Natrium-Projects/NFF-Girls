The Catoblepas is a bull-like monster which spawns on the Plain and Savanna biomes above Y=64.
<NEWLINE>
Its attack has a strong knockback, and gives Blindness and Slowness II when the difficulty is normal or above.
It's immune to Blindness effect.
<NEWLINE>
When it's slain, it can drop some Randomberry. This food can give you a random effect for a short time, either positive or negative.
