The Nightwalker is a golem creature found in the Badland biomes above Y=64. It attacks by shooting magical bullets,
giving Mining Fatigue I. As a magical golem, it's resistant to magic damage, but too heavy to float in water.
<NEWLINE>
When a Nightwalker is slain, it can drop some Randomberry. This food can give you a random effect for a short time,
either positive or negative. Also, it can occasionally drop some rare items like the Bottle o' Enchantment.
