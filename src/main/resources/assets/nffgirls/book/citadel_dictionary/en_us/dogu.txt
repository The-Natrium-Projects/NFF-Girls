The Dogu is an ancient automated golem warrior, spawning underground in the Jungle and Badland biomes.
<NEWLINE>
It spawns with Stong or Iron Sword, and doesn't have the sound of footsteps. Its attack has a strong knockback, and when
difficulty is normal or above, gives Slowness II and Mining Fatigue II.
<NEWLINE>
The Dogu can breathe underwater, and is immune to falling and Cactus damage. It takes halved projectile, explosion,
Powder Snow and falling blocks like the Anvil. Meanwhile, it takes double damage from Pickaxes.
<NEWLINE>
When a Dogu is slain, it may drop some Ancient Stones. This is a unique ingredient for Ancient Stone Blocks,
Ancient suit, Ancient Shield and Nemesis Blade.
<NEWLINE>
The Ancient suit is stronger than the Diamond but a bit weaker than the Netherite suit.
If you have 2 Ancient armors, you'll be immune to Slowness I, Mining Fatigue I and Weakness I.
Wearing 3 Ancient armors gives immunity to the Lv. II of the effects above, and wearing 4 will
make you immune to all level.
<NEWLINE>
The Ancient Shield can give the attacker Slowness I, Mining Fatigue I and Weakness I when blocking.
Also, it can boost your knockback resistance.
<NEWLINE>
The Nemesis Blade is a legendary weapon. It is stronger when your experience level is low. When it hits the enemy,
it will randomly give Slowness I, Mining Fatigue I or Weakness I for at most 10 seconds. However, if your XP level is too
high, your movement speed will be halved when using this weapon.
<NEWLINE>
When the weapon's level is above 1, you can hold RMB to shoot a magical bullet, consuming an XP level. The magical bullet
deals damage based on the weapon's level, and give the target Weakness II, Mining Fatigue II, slowness II and Wither II
for at most 15 seconds.
