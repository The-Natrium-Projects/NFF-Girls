The Magical Slime is a variant of the Slime. When a Slime Girl is slain, some Magical Slimes of the smallest size are
generated. The smallest Magical Slime can still deal damage.
It's resistant to falling and magic damage.
<NEWLINE>
It has 32 different colors like the Slime Girl, and drops Slime Balls when slain.
