To get friendly with a Jack o' Frost, you must drop the item on the ground. It will pick the item up, watch it for a few
seconds like the Piglin, and consume the item.
<NEWLINE>
The Jack o' Frost loves the Golden Apple, Golden Tropical Fish and HMaG berries. It also receives the Diamond, Emerald,
Lapis Lazuli and Blue Ice.
<NEWLINE>
After getting friendly, it has 4 Bauble slots. To make it immune to the hot biomes, it must equip a Resistance Amulet.
<NEWLINE>
The friendly Jack o' Frost can also only attack by shooting snowballs. Upon the XP level increasing, the snowball's
damage will be enhanced. When its XP level reaches a certain value, it will be able to shoot more snowballs each attack.
<NEWLINE>
The Jack o' Frost can be healed with the Pumpkin Pie, Snow Block and Snowball. A Golden Apple can fully recover its health.
<NEWLINE>
<NEWLINE>
Mod compat: it receives Ice Bomb (The Twilight Forest).
