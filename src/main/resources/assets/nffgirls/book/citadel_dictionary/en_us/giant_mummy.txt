The Giant Mummy is an ancient undead monster, found in the Desert biomes and the Desert Pyramids.
<NEWLINE>
When attacking, the Giant Mummy can break some blocks nearby, and give Slowness I and Hunger. It's resistant to projectiles.
<NEWLINE>
As an undead mob, the Giant Mummy burns under the sun.
<NEWLINE>
<NEWLINE>
When it's slain, it can drop some Randomberry. This food can give you a random effect for a short time, either positive
or negative.
