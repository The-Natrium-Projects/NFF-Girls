The Glaryad is a neutral mob found in the Lush Cave biomes. When attacked, it will alert other Glaryads nearby
like the Zombified Piglin. Its melee attack gives Slowness I.
<NEWLINE>
It loves moisture as a plant monster. When in water or rain, it will get Regeneration III effect. Oppositely,
it's weak to fire damage.
<NEWLINE>
You can hold a Mysterious Petal on your hand to attract the Glayrad. It will follow you if so, but can still be provoked
if attacked.
<NEWLINE>
When a Glayrad is slain, it drops special items from the Lush Cave, like Flowering Azalea and Moss block. It also has
a chance to drop some Mysterious Petals, a unique ingredient useful to make advanced items like Enchanted Golden
Apples.
