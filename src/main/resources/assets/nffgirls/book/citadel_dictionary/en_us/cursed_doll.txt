The Cursed Doll is a magical creature found in the Forest biomes above Y=64.
<NEWLINE>
It holds a Wooden or Stone Axe as weapon. The attack has a strong knockback, can disable the shield, and deals Slowness I.
<NEWLINE>
It burns under the sun, and is weak to fire damage. It falls slowly like the Chicken, and doesn't take
falling damage. It can step onto 1.5 blocks above.
<NEWLINE>
The Cursed Doll doesn't have the sound of footstep. Be aware of it's "surprise".
<NEWLINE>
It has 2 different textures.
