The Cursed Doll is an ownerless magical creature. It spawns in the Forest biomes above Y=64.
<NEWLINE>
It holds a Wooden or Stone Axe as weapon. The attack has a strong knockback and can disable the shield.
If the difficulty is normal or above, the attack deals Slowness I.
<NEWLINE>
It burns under the sun, and takes doubled fire damage. It falls slowly like the Chicken, and doesn't take
falling damage. It can step onto 1.5 blocks above.
<NEWLINE>
The Cursed Doll doesn't have the sound of footstep. Be aware of it's "surprise".
<NEWLINE>
It has 2 different textures.
