To get friendly with a Ghastly Seeker, you must first bring it to the overworld. When it's in the Overworld and can see
the sky, you can right-click to give it items.
<NEWLINE>
The Ghastly Seeker's favorite item is the Soul Cake Slice, and also accepts the Soul Powder and Soul Apple.
<NEWLINE>
After getting friendly, the Ghastly Seeker has 4 Bauble slots. Although not undead, it can equip the Soul Amulet.
<NEWLINE>
It requires ammo to shoot fireballs, either the Fire Charge and Blasting Bottle. The Fire Charge can make normal explosion
and not break blocks. The Blasting Bottle can make larger explosion and break blocks. The fireball explosion will be also
enhanced while its XP level increases.
<NEWLINE>
The Ghastly Seeker can be healed with the Gunpowder, Soul Powder and Soul Apple.
<NEWLINE>
If you have difficulties bringing it into the Overworld, you can try using the Reinforced Fishing Rod. This tool is similar
to the vanilla Fishing Rod, but much more capable in pulling entities. When it hooks an entity, by right-clicking you can
pull it multiple times without retrieving the hook, and the pulling force is also much stronger than the vanilla Fishing Rod.
This action doesn't consume extra durability. Shift + Right-click to retrieve the hook. The Reinforced Fishing Rod can be
repaired on the Anvil with the Reinforcing Chain. Indeed, you can fish with it as well.
