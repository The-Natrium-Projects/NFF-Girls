The Kobold is an outstanding miner underground, found in the dark places under Y=32. It spawns with a Stone Pickaxe.
Its attack gives Mining Fatigue I when the difficulty is normal or above.
<NEWLINE>
When it's slain, it has a chance to drop the Kobold Leather, a unique ingredient for making some items in this mod,
and brewing the Potion of Kobold's Wisdom. This potion provides Haste but also Weakness.
