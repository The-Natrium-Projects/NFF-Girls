The Dullahan is a scary creature with a headless appearance, found in the Dark Forest and Mountain biomes above Y=64.
It holds a Stone Sword as weapon, and the attack can gives Weakness I.
<NEWLINE>
As a powerful fighter, it's resistant to magic. It burns under the sun, although not undead.
<NEWLINE>
When a Dullahan is slain, it can drop some Soul Powder, a basic ingredient for many items in this mod. It also has
a chance to drop Randomberry. This food can give you a random effect for a short time, either positive or negative.
