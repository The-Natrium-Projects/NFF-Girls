The Dullahan patrols in the night. It spawns in the Dark Forest and Mountain biomes above Y=64.
It holds a Stone Sword as weapon, and the attack gives Weakness I when difficulty is normal or above.
<NEWLINE>
As a powerful fighter, it can halve the magic damage it takes. It burns under the sun, although not being undead.
<NEWLINE>
When a Dullahan is slain, it can drop some Soul Powder, a basic ingredient for many items in this mod. It also has
a chance to drop Randomberry. This food can give you a random effect for a short time, either positive or negative.
