The Harpy is a common creature found in the Plain, Savanna and Desert biomes above Y=64. Its attack has a strong
knockback. It falls slowly like the Chicken and is immune to falling damage. It can jump onto 1.5 blocks above.
<NEWLINE>
The Harpy has 6 different common colors depending on the biomes. Besides, there's another hidden color that can
be found anywhere but incredibly rarely.
