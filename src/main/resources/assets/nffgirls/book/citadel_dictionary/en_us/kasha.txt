The Kasha is a cat-like monster found in the Nether Wastes, Soul Sand Valley and Basalt Delta biomes. It stalks to its
prey silently and rush suddenly to attack. Its attack has a strong knockback and, if the difficulty is normal or above,
ignite the enemy.
<NEWLINE>
When spawn on Soul Sand or Soul Soil, it will become a variation of soul fire, and slightly stronger than normal ones.
<NEWLINE>
The Kasha is immune to fire, but takes damage in water or rain. It's also immune to falling damage and can jump onto
2 blocks above. It's hostile to the Chicken and baby Hoglin.
<NEWLINE>
When a Kasha is slain, it can drop some Soul Powder, a useful ingredient for many items in this mod.
