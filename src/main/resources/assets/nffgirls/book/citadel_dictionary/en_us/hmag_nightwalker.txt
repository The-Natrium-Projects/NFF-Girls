To get friendly with a Nightwalker, you must first set up a field to boost its power before giving items. It must be standing
on a Luminous Terracotta or Enhanced Luminous Terracotta. After giving, it will decay blocks it's standing on.
<NEWLINE>
The Luminous Terracotta and Enhanced Luminous Terracotta are Terracotta variants which emit 7 and 15 light level respectively.
The Luminous Terracotta can be obtained when a Nightwalker's magic ball hits a Glazed Terracotta block, or by crafting.
The Enhanced Luminous Terracotta can be obtained only when a Nightwalker's magic ball hits a Luminous Terracotta block.
<NEWLINE>
The Nightwalker loves the Cureberry, Randomberry, Experience Berry, and also receives the Ancient Stone and Clay Ball.
<NEWLINE>
After getting friendly, the Nightwalker has 4 Bauble slots, and an ammo slot.
<NEWLINE>
The ammo slot accepts Clay Ball and Ancient Stone. A Clay Ball can boost its magic balls' damage. An Ancient Stone can
further boost the damage, and expand the range of converting blocks. It can fire without ammo.
<NEWLINE>
The Nightwalker can be healed with the Clay Ball, Lapis Lazuli and Ancient Stone. A Golden Apple can fully recover its health.
