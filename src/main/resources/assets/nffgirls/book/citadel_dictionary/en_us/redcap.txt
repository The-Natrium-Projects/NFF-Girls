The redcap is a woodcutter creature found in the Dark Forest biomes above Y=64. It holds an axe as equipment, and
sometimes also wears Iron or Chainmail Boots. The Axe attack can disable the shield.
<NEWLINE>
When a Redcap is defeated, it can drop some Cureberry which can recover some health when eaten.
