The Ogre is a strong monster living in the cave, found below Y=0 in the Overworld.
<NEWLINE>
As a powerful creature, it can break blocks when attacking, causing a strong knockback and Slowness IV effect.
It's highly resistant to suffocation and Cactus, and also resistant to projectiles, explosions, fire, falling,
Powder Slow and falling blocks.
<NEWLINE>
When an Ogre is slain, it has a chance to drop some Ogre Horns. This unique item is the ingredient of the Potion of Ogre's
Strength, a potion giving Strength II and Haste I as well as Slowness II effects.
