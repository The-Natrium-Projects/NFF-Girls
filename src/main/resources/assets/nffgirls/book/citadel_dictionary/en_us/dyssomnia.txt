The Dyssomnia is the leader of Phantoms. It's stronger than the Phantom, larger and more fierce.
It can be found in open biomes like the Plain, and the spawn chance depends on the phase of the moon.
It's most likely to spawn under the full moon, and doesn't spawn at all under the new moon.
<NEWLINE>
The Dyssomnia attacks by shooting magical bullets and gives Blindness. Also, it can summon Phantoms during the combat.
<NEWLINE>
The Dyssomnia is immune to Blindness. Although undead, it doesn't burn under sun, but flies right
upward and disappears.
<NEWLINE>
When a Dyssomnia is slain, it can drop some Dyssomnia Skin. This is a rare unique ingredient for
insomnia items.
<NEWLINE>
The Sword of Insomnia can boost its strength as long as you don't sleep. After 3 days awake, the level and
the damage will increase by 1 each night you don't sleep. After sleeping, the level will return to 1.
You'll feel awake and cannot sleep when having this sword.
<NEWLINE>
The Fruit of Insomnia is a non-consuming food. It's level increase as long as you don't sleep, just like
the Sword of Insomnia.
<NEWLINE>
Each time eating, you'll recover 5 hunger points and 3 saturation, and get different effects:
<NEWLINE>
If the level is 1, you'll get poisoned, taking Hunger II, Blindness, Weakness II and Combustion. The item
will VANISH, and the cooldown time is 90s.
<NEWLINE>
Upon upgrading, it will provide stronger Regeneration, Night Vision and Absorption. You cannot sleep when
having this item.
