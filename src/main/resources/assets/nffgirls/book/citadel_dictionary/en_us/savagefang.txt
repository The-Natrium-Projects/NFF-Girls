The Savagefang is a fierce and hostile fish found in the Swamp and Jungle biomes. They attack their prey in groups, and
are hostile to players, animals, Villagers and even other hostile mobs. When the target is on the land, they can
also jump out of the water to attack.
<NEWLINE>
The Savagefang can drop Raw Savagefang Meat as a cookable food.
