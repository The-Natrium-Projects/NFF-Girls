To get friendly with a Slime Girl, you need to provide the Magical Gel by giving Magical Gel Balls or from Magical Gel
Bottles. The gel in the Magical Gel Bottle has a color. The closer its color is to the Slime Girl's color, the more
efficient it will be. The Magical Gel Ball is much less efficient (only 2-4% each), so it's recommended to use the bottle.
<NEWLINE>
The Magical Gel Ball is a throwable item. If a thrown Magical Gel Ball hits a large vanilla Slime, it has a chance (25%)
to generate a small Magical Slime of random color. If it hits a Slime Girl, it has a chance to generate a small Magical
Slime of the complementary color of the Slime Girl. Hitting a non-slime mob will give a knock back and Slowness II for 30s.
<NEWLINE>
The Magical Gel Bottle is an item for getting friendly with the Slime Girl, and modifying the color of friendly Slime Girls.
Right-click a Magical Gel Bottle to a small Magical Slime to collect it as gel of the same color. If there's already content
in the bottle, the color will be blended into the existing gel.
<NEWLINE>
Each bottle's capacity is 6. If you're trying to collect a Magical Slime with a full bottle, the extra unit of gel will
drop as a Magical Gel Ball after blending the color. Craft with an empty Magical Gel Bottle to divide the content into
2 bottles.
<NEWLINE>
Also, crafting a Magical Gel Bottle with an HMaG mob ingredient can also modify its color.
<NEWLINE>
When getting friendly with a Slime Girl with a Magical Gel Bottle, the progress provided by a single unit of gel depends
on how close the gel's color is to the Slime Girl's color. Note that the progress may drop if you're using a color too
far from its color. If the progress increases, it will generate green star particles; otherwise it generates angry particles.
<NEWLINE>
To estimate the Slime Girl's color, you can hit it with a Magical Gel Ball to generate a Magical Slime
of the complementary, collect it with an empty bottle and see the color.
<NEWLINE>
After getting friendly, the Slime Girl has 4 Bauble slots. Its color can be modified with the Magical Gel Bottle, and
after modification it has a chance to drop a Magical Gel Ball. Note that the magical gel's color will be blended with
the Slime Girl's original color.
<NEWLINE>
The Slime Girl can be healed with Slime Ball and Magical Gel Ball. A Cubic Nucleus can fully recover its health.
