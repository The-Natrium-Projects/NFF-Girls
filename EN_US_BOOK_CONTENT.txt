=== alraune.txt ===
<NEWLINE>
<NEWLINE>
<NEWLINE>
<NEWLINE>
The Alraune is a spirit of nature and plants, found in the Jungle biomes above Y=64.
<NEWLINE>
It can perform 2 types of attack depending on the distance to its enemies. When the enemy is close, it performs melee attack,
giving Poison II, Weakness III and Slowness III. If the enemy is far away, it can shoot poisonous seeds and give Poison I,
Weakness I and Slowness I.
<NEWLINE>
It is immune to Poison and Nausea effects.
<NEWLINE>
It loves moisture. When it's in water or rain, it will gain Regeneration III. Oppositely, it's weak to fire damage.
<NEWLINE>
When an Alraune is slain, it has a chance to drop Mysterious Petals. The unique ingredient is useful to make advanced items like Enchanted Golden Apples.
It can also drop some Cureberry which can recover some health when eaten.

=== crimson_slaughterer.txt ===
<NEWLINE>
<NEWLINE>
<NEWLINE>
<NEWLINE>
The Crimson Slaughterer is a dangerous creature found rarely in the Crimson Forest.
<NEWLINE>
It has higher attributes than most other mobs in this mod, and can step onto 3 blocks above, making it
dangerous to fight closely against it. Its attack can cause Hunger II, Slowness I and Mining Fatigue I.
It is arthropod, and weapons with Bane of Arthropod enchantment can deal it more damage.
<NEWLINE>
The Crimson Slaughterer is highly adaptive for extreme conditions. It's immune to fire and falling damage,
and Poison and Hunger effects. Also, it's resistant to projectiles, suffocation and Cactus.
<NEWLINE>
When it's slain, it has a chance to drop some Crimson Cuticula. This is a rare unique ingredient for Crimson Bow,
Nemesis Blade and some other items. It can also be used to make the Potion of Voracity which gives you Strength III
for a short time but also Hunger III for a longer time.
<NEWLINE>
The Crimson Bow is a bit powerful than vanilla Bow. Its power further increases along with your hunger value dropping.
<NEWLINE>
The Nemesis Blade is a legendary weapon. It is stronger when your experience level is low. When it hits the enemy,
it will randomly give Slowness I, Mining Fatigue I or Weakness I for at most 10 seconds. However, if your XP level is too
high, your movement speed will get slow when using this weapon.
<NEWLINE>
When the weapon's level is above 1, you can hold the right mouse button to shoot a magical bullet, consuming an XP level. The magical bullet
deals damage based on the weapon's level, and give the target Weakness II, Mining Fatigue II, slowness II and Wither II
for at most 15 seconds.

=== ender_executor.txt ===
<NEWLINE>

<NEWLINE>
<NEWLINE>
The Ender Executor spawns occasionally in place of the Ender Man in the. They are rare in the Overworld and the
Nether, but common in the End.
<NEWLINE>
It's health and attack attributes are much higher than the Ender Man. It spawns with a Stone Sword, and other

<NEWLINE>
Besides melee attack, the Ender Executor can also perform beam attack like the Guardian. The beam has very long tracking

The Ender Rage effect makes a player or mob proactively attacked by Ender Men and Ender Executors. Higher level can
increase the radius of provoking. Ender Men and Ender Executors are immune to this effect.
=== experience.txt ===
Monster girls can boost the experience level by killing mobs. When a monster girl kills a mob, the experience will
not drop as orbs, but directly be given to the monster girl. The XP amount requirement for each level is the same as players.
The experience can also repair the monster girl's equipment with Mending enchantment.
<NEWLINE>
Each experience level provides extra max HP by 1 and attack by 0.1. When it dies, a half of the experience will be lost.
<NEWLINE>
Note that the monster girls will not attack the unfriendly monster girls of the same type.
<NEWLINE>
When a monster girl dies, a Respawner will be generated and given to its owner, for respawning the monster girl.
=== favorability.txt ===
The favorability indicates the affinity between the player and monster girls. The value is between 0-100 and initially 50.
<NEWLINE>
You can slightly increase the favorability by doing things below:
<NEWLINE>
a) Sleeping beside your monster girls. It will boost favorability by 2 each time.
<NEWLINE>
b) Fight and defeat enemies with your monster girls.
<NEWLINE>
c) Heal your monster girls with items.
<NEWLINE>
Take care that activities below will GREATLY drop the favorability:
<NEWLINE>
a) Killing the monster girl by yourself will drop the favorability directly to 0!
<NEWLINE>
b) Attacking your monster girl. The favorability drop depends on the damage. A slight hit will not impact the favorability.
<NEWLINE>
c) Watching your monster girl dying in front you. It loses by 20.
<NEWLINE>
The monster girls' attack damage and trade price will be impacted by favorability. Besides, if the favorability is lower
than 5, the monster girl will refuse to follow you or attack your target.
<NEWLINE>
For Love and Peace, be friendly to your monster girls!

=== friending.txt ===
Usually, you must satisfy them to be friendly with a monster girl by giving them items.
<NEWLINE>
Most mobs requires you to right-click with the items in hand to give. Sometimes the action of giving
also requires additional conditions, such as effects or equipment. After giving, the "Friendly Progress"
increases depending on the item types.
<NEWLINE>
Some types of mobs have totally different methods to get friendly with, described under the entries of specific mobs.
<NEWLINE>
Generally, there are cheap and expensive items to give. Cheap items can slightly boost the progress, while the
expensive ones can significantly boost the progress, and sometimes even, if lucky enough, succeed immediately.
<NEWLINE>
Note that the giving action has a cool-down time. After giving successfully, the mob will generate glint (green stars) particles.
If failed due to the cool-down, it will generate black smoke particles. Each time the progress increases by 20%, the mob
will generate a heart particle.
<NEWLINE>
If other conditions are satisfied, but you've already been hostile to the mob, it will generate anger particles.
<NEWLINE>
Some hostile behaviors can interrupt the progress. Once interrupted, the progress will drop back to 0, and you have to
start from the beginning. At this time the mob will also generate anger particles.
<NEWLINE>
Some mobs are more alert, and you need to put the present on the ground. They will pick them up like Piglins,
hold the item for a few seconds, consume it and boost the progress.
<NEWLINE>
Required item types are shown in the corresponding JEI tab. Note that mobs using unconventional friending processes
are not shown in JEI, and can be found in this guidebook.

=== hmag_creeper_girl.txt ===
<NEWLINE>
<NEWLINE>
<NEWLINE>
<NEWLINE>
To get friendly with a Creeper Girl, first you must right-click to give it items. It loves the Particle of Lightning
best, and also receives the TNT and Gunpowder.
<NEWLINE>
When the progress is full, it will stop moving, swell for 4 second with massive green star and smoke particles.
After the 4 seconds, it will make a HUGE EXPLOSION (power 12). During this process, you must not get over 8 blocks away
from the Creeper Girl, or the process will be interrupted, and you have to restart from giving items. You must survive
from the explosion no matter how, and it will finally get friendly with you.
<NEWLINE>

you can use a Particle of Lightning by Shift + Right-click to charge it, and its next explosion will have double power.
Shift + Right-click your charged Creeper Girl to uncharge it and recover a Particle of Lightning.
<NEWLINE>
Its explosions will not hurt itself, but can hurt any players and mobs. The explosions will not kill the owner's other
monster girls or tamed animals, but can kill its owner.
<NEWLINE>
The Creeper Girl can be healed with the Redstone, Gunpowder and Redstone Block.

=== interaction.txt ===
To interact with a monster girl, you need a Commanding Wand. Right click the mob with a Commanding Wand to switch its
behaviors among Waiting, Following and Wandering. They will attack mobs which are attacking it or the owner, or the
owner is attacking.
<NEWLINE>
Some items can be used to heal the monster girl. Hold the item on the main hand and right-click the mob to heal.
Healing items are shown in the corresponding JEI tab.
<NEWLINE>
Shift + Right click with a Commanding Wand to open the GUI of the monster girl. The GUI shows basic data and attributes
of the mob. Also, you can customize the mob's inventory. Some mobs can equip weapons and/or armors to boost the attributes,
and most enchantments can also take effects.
<NEWLINE>
Most mobs can equip "Baubles" in the slots with the gem icon. These slots accept only specific Bauble items and provide
effects depending on item types. Most Bauble effects are repeatable.
<NEWLINE>
Right click with bare hand or the Evil Gem to trade with the mobs. The Evil Gem is the currency in the trade with
monster girls. Trade entries are shown in the corresponding JEI tab.
<NEWLINE>
Each mob has 10 trade entries. Different mobs have different trade entries, many in which provide rare items.
Some trade entries are unlocked only when the mob reaches a specific XP level. When a trade entry is out-of-stock,
it will take some time to restock. Entries of higher level will take more time to restock.
<NEWLINE>
You can get "Trade Points" when trading with a monster girl, and consume 128 Trade Points for a "Trade Introduction Letter".
The letter can be given to another monster girl to refresh its trade entries. Also, you'll get discount depending on
your favorability with the mob.

=== monolith.txt ===
<NEWLINE>
<NEWLINE>
<NEWLINE>
<NEWLINE>
The Monolith is an ancient automated defensive device, found rarely in the Small End Islands and End Barrens, and
commonly in the End City.
<NEWLINE>
The Monolith doesn't move when it doesn't detect a player. It can perform 2 types of distant attacks depending on the
distance of the enemy. When the enemy is far away, it performs beam attack like the Guardian, and gives Weakness I,
Slowness I and Mining Fatigue I. When the enemy is nearby, it sprays smoke, causing Weakness II, Blindness,
Slowness II, Mining Fatigue II and knockback.
<NEWLINE>
The Monolith is resistant to fire, explosion, magic and Cactus, but weak to the Pickaxe.
<NEWLINE>
When a Monolith is slain, it drops some Ancient Stones. This is a unique ingredient for Ancient Stone Blocks,
Ancient suit, Ancient Shield and Nemesis Blade.
<NEWLINE>
The Ancient suit is stronger than the Diamond but a bit weaker than the Netherite suit.
If you have 2 Ancient armors, you'll be immune to Slowness I, Mining Fatigue I and Weakness I.
Wearing 3 Ancient armors gives immunity to the Lv. II of the effects above, and wearing 4 will
make you immune to all level.
<NEWLINE>
The Ancient Shield can give the attacker Slowness I, Mining Fatigue I and Weakness I when blocking.
Also, it can boost your knockback resistance.
<NEWLINE>
The Nemesis Blade is a legendary weapon. It is stronger when your experience level is low. When it hits the enemy,
it will randomly give Slowness I, Mining Fatigue I or Weakness I for at most 10 seconds. However, if your XP level is too
high, your movement speed will be halved when using this weapon.
<NEWLINE>
When the weapon's level is above 1, you can hold RMB to shoot a magical bullet, consuming an XP level. The magical bullet
deals damage based on the weapon's level, and give the target Weakness II, Mining Fatigue II, slowness II and Wither II
for at most 15 seconds.
